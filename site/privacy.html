Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

This policy describes how the app Our Open Cookbook (the “app”) processes your data. The short version: your recipes are stored on your own device, are only synced to your own Google Drive account if you enable it yourself, and are never sold or shared with third parties.

1. Who is responsible

The app is developed by Daniel Hansson. Contact: ouropencookbook@neocreo.net

2. What data is processed

Stored on your device

  • Recipes, cookbooks, ingredients, instructions, notes, and images that you create or import yourself. Storage is the app's local database on the device (IndexedDB in the app's data space).
  • App settings (theme, OCR language choice) in the app's local storage.
  • A randomly generated, anonymous device identifier (UUID) used to keep track of versions during synchronization. It is not linked to your identity.

Google Drive synchronization (only if you enable it)

If you sign in with Google and start a sync, your recipes and cookbooks are written as JSON files to the folder /OurOpenCookbook in your own Google Drive account. The app uses the Google permission drive.file — it can only read and write files the app itself has created, never other content in your Drive. Sign-in happens via Google OAuth; the access token is kept only temporarily in memory during the session and is not stored permanently. The app does not read your email address or other profile information.

Camera and images

The app uses the camera only so that you can photograph a recipe page for OCR reading. Images are processed locally in the app (text recognition happens on the device). An image only becomes part of a recipe when you save it, and then follows the recipe — including Drive sync, if enabled.

3. Is data shared with third parties?

No. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and never sells data. Your data leaves your device only in the form of a sync to your own Google Drive account, which you initiate and can disconnect or clear at any time. This website (opencookbook.online) sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

4. How long is data kept

Until you delete it. Delete a recipe in the app and it is removed from the device and, at the next sync, also from Drive. If you uninstall the app, the local database is deleted. Files in your Drive are deleted by you via Google Drive.

5. Your rights

Since the app does not collect data about you, there is no register to request extracts from. You have full control: delete, export (JSON/Markdown in the app), or disconnect Drive sync whenever you want.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, the date above and the published version at this URL are updated.

8. Contact

Questions about the policy or the app: ouropencookbook@neocreo.net.
